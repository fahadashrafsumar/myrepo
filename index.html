About me

FS

I’m Fahad, an infrastructure guy from Karachi with 7 years of keeping servers, networks and backups alive. It’s the kind of work nobody notices until it breaks. These days I manage IT infrastructure and networks for a large organization, where I’ve moved an entire DR site, led a big security remediation push, and replaced a lot of “someone checks this by hand every morning” with scripts that do it for me.

Lately I’ve been on a mission to drag on-prem infrastructure into the modern world: containers, Kubernetes, Azure, and self-hosted AI that keeps company data where it belongs. I’m studying for AZ-104, building internal web apps nobody asked me to build, and running LLMs on hardware that really wishes it had a GPU.

If it can be automated, I’ll probably automate it. If it can’t, I’ll probably try anyway.
